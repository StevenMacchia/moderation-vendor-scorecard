# Vendor scoring rubric

Score each moderation vendor from 1 to 5 on eight criteria. Weights below are the defaults; adjust them to your program. A score of 2 or lower on a criterion marked **minimum 3** rules a vendor out, whatever its total.

## Decision quality · weight 20

> Will their reviewers make the same calls your experts would?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **1** | No QA data they'll share, or low agreement with your experts. |
| **3** | Solid agreement on their own QA, but not tested on your policies. |
| **5** | High agreement in a blind pilot on your data, measured by your team. |

**Questions to ask in your RFP**

- What QA agreement rate do you achieve on comparable policies, and who measures it?
- Will you run a paid pilot on our data with blind QA?
- How do you calibrate reviewers when our policy changes?

## Reviewer wellness · weight 15 · minimum 3

> Will they protect the people who look at the worst content?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **1** | No exposure limits or counseling, and attrition they won't disclose. |
| **3** | Counseling and some exposure limits, but not enforced per person. |
| **5** | Hard daily exposure caps per person, licensed counseling during and after employment, blurring tools by default. |

**Questions to ask in your RFP**

- What is the maximum daily exposure to graphic content per moderator?
- Is licensed counseling available, including after someone leaves?
- What was annual attrition for T&S staff last year?
- Do you use content blurring and grayscale tooling by default?

## Language and market coverage · weight 15

> Can they understand your users in every language and market?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **1** | Machine translation or non-native reviewers for key markets. |
| **3** | Native speakers for major languages, with gaps in dialects and smaller markets. |
| **5** | Native, in-market reviewers for every language you need, with local cultural knowledge. |

**Questions to ask in your RFP**

- Which of our languages do you cover with native speakers, not translation?
- How do you handle regional dialects and slang?
- Where are reviewers located for each market?

## Surge capacity · weight 10

> Can they scale up fast when something goes wrong?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **1** | Weeks to add capacity and no incident playbook. |
| **3** | Can add 20 to 30% within a couple of weeks, with some quality dip. |
| **5** | Proven surge within days, a trained reserve, and follow-the-sun coverage. |

**Questions to ask in your RFP**

- How fast can you add 30% capacity, and how is it trained?
- What happened in your last surge event?
- Do you offer follow-the-sun coverage?

## Security and privacy · weight 15 · minimum 3

> Can you trust them with your users' data?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **1** | No certifications, and reviewers on personal devices. |
| **3** | SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified, with limited access logging. |
| **5** | Certified, managed devices or clean rooms, audited access, and a documented process for illegal content. |

**Questions to ask in your RFP**

- Do you hold SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification?
- Do reviewers work on managed devices or in clean rooms?
- How is access to personal data logged and audited?
- How do you handle legally restricted content such as CSAM referrals?

## Cost · weight 10

> What will it really cost once everything is included?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **1** | Opaque pricing, heavy minimums and paid ramp time. |
| **3** | Clear per-decision or per-hour pricing with some extras. |
| **5** | Transparent all-in pricing that stays competitive once rework and appeals are counted. |

**Questions to ask in your RFP**

- What is the price per decision or per hour, and what is excluded?
- How are pilot, training and ramp time billed?
- Are there minimum volume commitments?

## Tooling and integration · weight 5

> Will they work inside your tools and share data back?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **1** | Must use their own tool, with no data back to you. |
| **3** | Can use your tool with some setup, and periodic exports. |
| **5** | Works inside your review stack and sends decision data back through an API. |

**Questions to ask in your RFP**

- Can you work in our review tool, or do you require your own?
- Can you share decision data back to us via API?

## Reporting transparency · weight 10

> Will you be able to see how they're really performing?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **1** | Monthly summary slides only. |
| **3** | Regular dashboards with QA summaries. |
| **5** | Live dashboards, raw QA samples and fast escalation of quality misses. |

**Questions to ask in your RFP**

- What dashboards do we get, and how often?
- Will you share raw QA samples, not just summaries?
- How are quality misses escalated to us?
